## Missing product capabilities and dependencies

**Priceable Entity and context management (DATA, pp83–98).** SKU is not the universal model. Product can be directly priced; service and another organisation-defined offering are minimum types. Categories/groups select entities; dimensions qualify economic records without automatically creating a new entity. Current backend Product is a parent and all economic records require SKU identity. Sample Catalog is read-only; manual economic entry and entity archival commands are not present. Archival flags and a MANUAL source enum do not establish those journeys. Relevant reusable dimensions have partial source support, but arbitrary examples must not be turned into mandatory individual integrations or hierarchy levels.

Cost basis is organisation-supplied meaning, not automatically one universal accounting cost. Multiple bases/locations/periods are required when applicable, and any logistics/fees/other components used in pricing must have a defined compatible input basis rather than silently being folded into an arbitrary “current cost.” The PRD does **not** require a full cost-accounting engine, enterprise overhead allocation or reconstruction of incomplete accounting values (DATA-004, pp86–88).

**Initiative management (INIT, pp100–110).** There is no distinct persistent Pricing Initiative resource, route or lifecycle. Title/owner/next-step foundations belong to Proposal work, not a broader accountable container with objective/free-text rationale, type, priority, collaborators, target/expiry dates, typed Assumptions, readiness, hold/cancel/completion and objective-to-outcome history. A ChangeSet is editable pricing material; a Proposal is the formal decision object. Neither can simply be renamed to satisfy INIT-021.

**Builder (BUILD, pp112–126).** Three prepared frontend increase outcomes and four backend adjustment operators do not implement bounded quantitative/logical pricing construction. User pricing action/strategy, input/operator/value, conditions, thresholds, assumptions, initiative constraints and effective periods need their own versioned structured representation. The existing organisational MarginRule engine is useful but must not become a substitute for builder-owned strategy logic. New/manual/qualitative values must remain distinguishable from deterministic calculation. Human-readable logic, provenance, controlled reuse and change detection depend on these contracts.

**Analysis and Scenarios (ANALYSIS, pp128–146).** No named independent Scenario create/edit/compare/select/history model exists. Fixture “scenario” switches and sequential Preview/Proposal versions are not user alternatives. Gross-profit and markup outputs, user target-margin/target-markup actions, explicit cost assumptions and full pre-/post-rounding trace are incomplete. Guardrail floor arithmetic is not itself a target-price builder action. Scenario isolation must preserve live prices and approvals until deliberate human progression; selection is not approval.

**AI and selected external connectivity.** BUILD-019 and ANALYSIS-020 expressly require invocable assistive AI. Proposed changes need human application and attributable history; interpretation cannot alter deterministic calculations. DATA-021 requires bounded external integration capability, and says at least one validated-pilot connector **should** be supported. No chosen business/productivity connector was found; CSV and standalone FX conversion do not satisfy that feature. Broad connector coverage, universal bidirectional sync and autonomous collection are not required.

Dependency order: (1) canonical entity/context resolver and safe economics access; (2) Initiative/Assumption and controlled source/version contracts; (3) bounded Builder and shared complete deterministic engine; (4) isolated Scenarios/comparison and analytical completeness; (5) Proposal readiness consumption with authoritative multi-Guardrail results; (6) live adapter, human-controlled AI and selected connector, with contract/integration proof. Independent work can proceed in parallel, but downstream “ready” claims cannot bypass unresolved inputs/version/authority contracts.

## Interpretation and handoff contradictions

PRD pp73–74 control interpretation: **must/must not** applies whenever its stated condition exists; **should** is expected MVP behavior and needs an explicit product decision to defer without breaking mandatory/core integrity; **may** alone is optional; examples do not expand scope. No explicit deferral decision was found for this domain, so none is silently assigned P3. Duplication, general reuse, autosave and analysis export are marked P2 where their leading behavior is should; their safety/continuity conditions remain mandatory when applicable.

The PRD permits structured qualitative/custom pricing work but does not require arbitrary executable formulas, user-authored code, free-form workflow graphs, autonomous pricing, elasticity modelling, demand forecasting, psychological price-ending optimisation or a full cost-accounting platform (pp65–68; DATA-004; ANALYSIS-023). “Simulation” here means supported deterministic Scenario evaluation and explicit manual/assumed inputs, not an inferred demand model.

Older frontend handoffs are implementation context, not authority to narrow this replacement PRD. In particular, [FRONTEND_JOURNEY_GAPS AI deferral](../../../../apps/workspace/docs/FRONTEND_JOURNEY_GAPS.md#L84) and [BACKEND_ONBOARDING_READINESS AI row](../../../../apps/workspace/docs/BACKEND_ONBOARDING_READINESS.md#L92) conflict with the current mandatory assistive-AI requirements. Their narrow sample journey descriptions remain useful when read literally, but do not establish Initiative/Scenario/MVP completeness. Likewise, any older claim that gateway service-token support is wholly absent is stale: `src/gateway/src/clients/service-auth.ts` exists in current source; separate role/field authorisation gaps still need evaluation.
