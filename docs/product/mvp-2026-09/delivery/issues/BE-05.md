## Outcome

Implement immutable submitted snapshots, independent controlled/concurrency versions and canonical states/validity. Enforce actual-submitter maker-checker, ordered scoped decisions, distinct outcomes and default-disabled policy authorisation. Emit events; do not call Governance as a mutation shortcut.
