# CT-01 — Ratify product decisions and PRD dispositions

Track: **contracts** · Priority: **P0** · Milestone: **M0** · Kind: **planning-task** · Initial readiness: **ready-for-plan**

GitHub: https://github.com/Owomide-Engineering/owomide_core/issues/51

## Outcome

Record D-01–08 with accountable decider, accepted option, rationale, affected requirements and explicit expected/optional deferrals. The report is accepted; unresolved role/AI/pilot choices are not silently settled.

Drafting may prepare decision options and a row-level Must/Should/May/Not Required disposition inventory. Only an authorised human can ratify an option or scope deviation. Preserve historical guidance and audit findings; do not rewrite them as if the new decision had always applied.

## Dependencies and stop conditions

Depends on: none for drafting.
D-01–08 as applicable; drafting is allowed, ratification remains human.

A planning task may draft before ratification. A blocked implementation package must not start coding. Split a large package into bounded child tasks before implementation; carry over its requirement facets and do not close the parent prematurely.

## Requirement ownership

Cross-cutting decision/contract dependency: all affected rows remain owned by their implementation and integration packages.

This is facet ownership, not whole-requirement completion. Optional/excluded/Should rows require the correct disposition; do not build every illustrative example.

## Non-goals

- No new repository or unrelated redesign.
- No production writes, permission grants, deployment or automatic merge.
- No fixture behaviour labelled live; preserve live-adapter release gates.
- No arbitrary connectors, unrestricted automation or AI decision authority.
