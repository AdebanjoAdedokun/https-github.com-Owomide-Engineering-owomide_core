# V1 30-day release control plan

**Planning target:** 27 September–27 October 2026 (30 calendar days)

**Status:** Planning and release control only. This document does not approve implementation, ratify a product decision, or authorize a live operation.

## Release posture

**A full replacement-PRD V1 is not currently credible or guaranteed within 30 days.** The accepted delivery index marks all FE, BE and IN implementation packages blocked; only CT planning work is initially ready-for-plan, with CT-02 gated by CT-01. Ready to plan is not permission to implement. This is a dependency-controlled schedule, not a completion forecast.

A narrower pilot is not an assumed shortcut. It requires explicit, recorded, authorized decisions for **D-01, D-04, D-05, D-06 and D-07**. The accountable owners—not this plan or an implementer—must choose and record them. Other decisions (including role/grant and requirement-disposition decisions) remain gates wherever the selected scope depends on them. Until the necessary decisions and CT-02 contracts are ratified, dependent implementation stays blocked.

The frontend remains sample-backed until reviewed live adapters and service gates pass. Sample UI behaviour or passing fixture tests are not live-release proof. Do not remove the live-adapter gate or use a fixture as a fallback for a live failure. The 379 requirement rows and their frontend/backend/integration evidence facets are coverage and disposition work—not a percentage-complete metric.

## Day-2 scope and decision checkpoint — 29 September 2026

At 48 hours from the 27 September start, the product owner and relevant accountable leads review the recorded CT-01/CT-02 status and decide whether a viable target can be baselined. They must explicitly resolve or keep open **D-01, D-04, D-05, D-06 and D-07** for a proposed narrower pilot. Also identify any D-02/D-03/D-08 dispositions that the chosen permissions, collaboration or requirement scope requires. Record each decision in the decision register using its required fields; a draft, issue, PR, default value or verbal assumption is not ratification.

This checkpoint is a stop/go for planning the selected scope, not a deadline that turns unknowns into defaults. If any required decision or contract remains unratified, keep dependent implementation blocked, continue only authorized decision/contract planning, and take a no-go/rebaseline recommendation to the accountable owner. Do not silently reduce scope, relax evidence or describe a sample flow as a pilot.

## Dependency-gated parallelism

Parallel work is allowed only after the exact issue dependencies and relevant decision/contract gates are satisfied. A package being listed earlier in the sequence does not waive its gate.

- **Gate A — decisions and contracts:** CT-01 must precede CT-02. After CT-02 is accepted and the affected access/context decisions are recorded, FE-01/FE-02 and BE-01 may proceed in parallel in their separate ownership areas.
- **Gate B — foundational domains:** FE-03 waits for FE-01/02; BE-02 waits for BE-01, and BE-03 follows BE-02. Once their explicit prerequisites clear, the FE-03 Initiative work and BE-02/03 contextual pricing work may proceed concurrently. FE-04 then precedes FE-05; BE-04 waits for BE-01/03.
- **Gate C — controlled lifecycle:** FE-06 waits for FE-03/04; FE-07 precedes FE-08. BE-05 waits for BE-01/03/04 and D-05. Only after BE-05 may BE-06 and BE-07 run concurrently (each with its own upstream gates); FE-09 still waits for FE-08 and D-06.
- **Gate D — downstream work:** BE-08 and BE-09 may run in parallel only after their separately listed BE dependencies and D-01 where AI applies. FE-10/11 remain gated by their listed FE dependencies and D-01. IN-01 waits for all FE-01–05 and BE-01–04; IN-02 and IN-03 can overlap only after each full dependency list and pilot/connector decision is satisfied. IN-04 is last.

Use one reviewed branch/worktree per executable task and avoid overlapping edits. Apply the [agent execution contract](AGENT_PLAYBOOK.md), including issue claim/coordination, bounded plans, independent review and facet-specific handoff evidence.

## Measurable go/no-go checklist

The release owner records **GO** only when every applicable item below is evidenced and the accountable owners have approved the scope. Otherwise the outcome is **NO-GO / rebaseline**.

- [ ] The decision register records the authorized D-01/D-04/D-05/D-06/D-07 choices required for the proposed full V1 or narrower pilot, plus all other affected role, policy and Must/Should/May dispositions; no open decision is treated as a default.
- [ ] CT-02's applicable canonical contracts, version/authority rules, data context and migration/compatibility boundaries are accepted before dependent runtime behaviour.
- [ ] Every required package for the declared target meets its own issue acceptance criteria, has independent review and passing relevant CI, and reports tests/evidence and limitations. Storage changes include migration/restart/rollback evidence where applicable.
- [ ] Backend and connected acceptance evidence covers authorized success as well as denied, missing/ambiguous, stale/version, replay/retry, partial/unknown and relevant restart paths; applicable population handling includes the issue's >500-member case.
- [ ] A real, separately authorized multi-user path through Gateway and service boundaries is verified. For IN-03, one decision-selected external integration proves exact-scope outcomes; transport acknowledgement alone is insufficient.
- [ ] Affected UI has browser evidence at desktop/mobile (and applicable tablet/zoom) for keyboard/focus, open controls and scrolling. Sample/fixture labels remain explicit; fixture tests are not counted as backend or live-integration proof, and live errors never fall back to fixtures.
- [ ] IN-04 reconciles all **379** requirement rows across frontend, backend and integration evidence facets, with approved dispositions for expected deferrals and optional/excluded rows. This is a row-by-row gate, **not a percentage-complete target**.
- [ ] No unresolved severity-one release blocker, unauthorized price operation, permission grant, production deployment or connector credential change remains; the actual operational authority is separately recorded.

## If decisions or contracts are not ratified

Keep all dependent implementation packages blocked. Continue only bounded decision and contract work that is authorized; preserve unresolved states, source context and existing live-adapter protections. At the 48-hour checkpoint and each weekly review, record the blocker, accountable role, affected IDs and next decision date. If the gate is still unmet, recommend **NO-GO for 27 October** and request an explicitly approved new date or scope baseline. A narrower pilot must still pass its authorized D-01/D-04/D-05/D-06/D-07 decisions and connected evidence; no implementer may make those choices for the owner.
